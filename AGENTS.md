# VIRTURA Collective Agent Rules

## Scope

This repo is the public team frontstage for VIRTURA Collective.

Use it for:

- team identity
- people pages
- selected team works
- programs
- collaboration routes
- `virtura.space`

Do not use it for Ewan's personal portfolio or internal archive/cache text.

## Final Quality Gate

Final Collective pages must read as finished public pages, not production notes.

Before treating HTML, Markdown, people pages, work pages, media kit text, or demos as final, run:

`python3 /Users/ewanqian/.codex/skills/virtura-final-quality-gate/scripts/final_quality_gate.py <paths>`

Final pages must not include:

- draft / review / approval language
- proof / evidence / source-path language
- TODO / needs / fix / verify language
- "if you want to read..." instructions
- internal boundary warnings such as "not team-owned"

Use public labels instead:

- `Independent Work`
- `Artist Context`
- `Collaboration`
- `Credits`
- `Media Kit`
- `Coming Soon`

## Boundary

- Kashiwa / TITAN stays portfolio / external collaboration context, not Collective team context.
- Murphy / Sihui independent works stay `member_independent_work` until formal work pages with credits and rights exist.
- People pages can show roles and routes, but team role is not automatic ownership.
